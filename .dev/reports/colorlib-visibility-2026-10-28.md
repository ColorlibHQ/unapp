### Ready-to-adapt copy for the first three

**Photography:** Unapp is our free block theme with a Photographer starter built around images, commissions and enquiries. Its starter includes selected work, the approach and a project contact path, with supporting pages when you want them. Replace the illustrative images with your own photographs in WordPress's native editor; the guided content pass takes you to each image. No page builder is required.

**Charity:** Unapp's free Nonprofit starter gives a community organisation a homepage plus programme, about, support and contact pages. It is edited with native WordPress blocks and includes volunteering and giving enquiries. Point giving buttons at your published donation destination when ready; the theme itself does not process donations or provide recurring-giving software.

**Personal:** Unapp offers Portfolio and Coach & Consultant starters for independent professionals. Start with one page or add supporting pages for your work, approach and enquiries. The setup preview shows your name, details and main action before building, and real service-card fields carry your offer into the page. All nineteen starters are free; the theme uses the native Site Editor.

## Editorial requirements when adding a new entry

Use the actual niche demo and a current screenshot, plus the [product page](https://colorlib.com/wp/themes/unapp/) and [visual getting-started guide](https://docs.colorlib.com/unapp/getting-started/). Disclose that Colorlib maintains Unapp. Keep its self-hosted distribution distinct from WordPress.org install/rating data; no directory install count, star rating or comparable speed score has been established for these proposed entries.

When an entry is added, synchronize the article's visible total, SEO title/description, contents list, numbering and any comparison/structured-data item list. Include Unapp in an existing benchmark table only after running that article's same test protocol. State the relevant feature boundaries: forms use optional plugins, Shop uses WooCommerce, donation/booking/ticket processing is separate, and advanced gallery features are not supplied by the starter.

The strongest first batch is Photography, Charity, Personal and eCommerce. After those, add Practice and Conference to the corresponding free-theme articles, then expand the portfolio/one-page/gallery coverage. Local-service topics can feature Home Services, Cleaning and Salon/Barber when their actual editorial scope fits those starters.
